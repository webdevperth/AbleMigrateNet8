To tailor the Coaching Program to your specific needs we're running a 360 Feedback Process. This involves gathering feedback from yourself as well as your peers, managers and direct-reports on how you are going in your role. We call them "raters".

"Raters" might sound scary, but this is not a performance review. It's a chance for you to identify key strengths and areas for development and, with the support of your colleagues, enhance your skills as a leader.

This survey should only take 15 minutes to complete. Please complete this survey before [CloseDate] and invite your raters to complete.

Inviting Raters

You will invite raters at the end of the survey. However, it's best to have a chat with your potential raters beforehand, asking them if they could give youfeedback. If you prefer to, you can also email your potential raters using this email template.

For accurate feedback, invite at least 8 raters including, peers, managers and direct-reports.
